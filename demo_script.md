# Demo script (about 2 minutes)

Roughly 300 words at an easy speaking pace, which lands at about 2:00. The times give room up to 2:15.
Each section matches a slide in `demo_slides.pdf`.

---

**Slide 1: The question (0:00 – 0:15)**

Hi, we're Uni Trifecta. Quick question: what should a two-bedroom flat with parking in Zetland rent for?
The suburb median says $1,165 a week. But real adverts ask anywhere from $978 to $1,500, depending on the building.

**Slide 2: What we found (0:15 – 0:35)**

And that's the big finding. The building a flat is in sets its rent. The floor barely matters, about a dollar
fifty a week per floor. A car space is worth around $76 a week. So a suburb average misses by about $100 a week.

**Slide 3: How it works (0:35 – 1:05)**

So we price every one of the 65,777 flats in the City of Sydney building by building. We start with the closest
evidence we have. If the flat itself was advertised before, its own past rents count most. Then its building,
matched on bedrooms, bathrooms and parking. If the building has barely advertised, we lean on the nearest
buildings, and only then on the suburb. Older adverts are brought up to July 2026 prices, and every flat gets two
rents: with a car space and without.

**Slide 4: Does it work? (1:05 – 1:30)**

We only test on adverts the model hasn't seen yet. Across twenty rolling two-month tests, the typical miss is
about $51 a week. On May to July this year it was $45, against $100 for the suburb method. About three in four
estimates land within $100.

**Slide 5: Live demo (1:30 – 1:55)**

And we turned it into something tenants can use. Type your address, say 841/2C Defries Avenue, Zetland.
Bedrooms and parking fill in from the records. Enter what you pay, and it shows how you compare with similar
flats, and how confident the estimate is.

**Slide 6: What's next (1:55 – 2:10)**

It's weakest on buildings that have never advertised and on renovated flats asking way above their building.
Next, we'd add Microburbs data like transport and schools for those. Building by building, not suburb by suburb.
Thanks!

## Recording tips

- On slide 5, switch to the live site at microburbsdataanalysis.vercel.app, type the address, choose it, and enter a
  rent such as $900.
- If you run long, cut the sentence about older adverts on slide 3. It saves about 8 seconds.
- Slide 7 is a backup for judges' questions and isn't part of the talk.
