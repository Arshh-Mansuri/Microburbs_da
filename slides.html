Backup for questions

The technical details, if asked.

  • Estimate: weighted median of past asking rents. Older asks are brought forward with a citywide quarterly index and count half as much every 3 years.
  • Order of evidence: own past asks (75% weight) > building (same bedrooms and parking; bathrooms if 3+ adverts) > 10 nearest buildings within 1 km (weight halves every 500 m) > suburb > bedrooms.
  • Thin buildings are pulled toward their neighbours. Each parking side is adjusted by its own evidence.
  • Missing parking side: other rent plus or minus the local car-space gap, capped at 25% of rent.
  • Testing: settings chosen on rolling tests before May 2026. May to July was looked at across versions, so the rolling $51 is the fairer guide.
  • Hard cases (rolling tests): no own history $63 typical miss; no usable building history $90.
  • Tried and dropped: floor adjustment, forcing parking above no parking, a growth lift after 31 July.
