What moved the miss

Every row is scored on the same 3,954 adverts dated 1 May to 31 July 2026. Groups are built only from adverts dated by 30 April 2026. A suburb estimate gives every flat in a suburb the same rent. Swapping the suburb for the flat's own building is what closes the gap, and bringing older asks forward to April 2026 is what makes the building comparison reach 95% of flats instead of a quarter of them.

Bringing older asks forward

An ask from 2023Q2 cannot be pooled with one from 2026Q2 as it stands. Each is scaled by how the median weekly ask across the city moved between its quarter and April 2026, then weighted down as it ages, with a three year half life. Across this window the median ask moved +1%.

What a car space adds

Measured on groups that advertised both answers. Where a building has only ever advertised one, the other rent is that one plus this gap, capped so a car space never lowers the ask. Where a building advertised both, its own two medians stand, which is why 6,323 flats come out with parking priced lower.

What this is built from
